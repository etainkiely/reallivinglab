How the score is built. Identification is AI-assisted and shown with its confidence; anything uncertain is marked "to be confirmed" for a human recorder before it counts. Rarity is anchored to the species' IUCN Red List category, bioindicator value to documented sensitivity. The Hero Rating is computed here by a fixed formula (30% role, 25% bioindicator, 20% rarity, 15% legacy, 10% sensitivity), so every card is consistent. Ratings tell a story; they are not a measurement.
