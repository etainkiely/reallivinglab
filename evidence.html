Life at REAL

A growing nature record — not our own Red List.

REAL can record what is observed locally while relying on official Irish and international conservation sources for formal status. Verification stays separate from storytelling.

Student or community submissions can move through a simple path: submit → check → verify → publish.

Privacy & provenance

Open where useful. Protected where necessary.

Private project sites do not need precise public pins. Children and participants do not need to become public content. Evidence can be transparent about method while protecting location and identity.
