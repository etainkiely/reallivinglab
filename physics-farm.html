Physic(s) Farm

Care enough to notice. Measure enough to know.
Year 0 · Baseline

Six acres. Not yet a model landscape. That is the point.

Physic(s) Farm begins with a poorly managed six-acre field and a commitment to observe before claiming success. The project will ask what is already here, what changes under different management and what evidence would justify the next intervention.

The baseline matters. Future photographs, species observations, soil measures, water conditions and management decisions can be compared back to what was actually present at the start.

PHYSIC

Care, plants, restoration

Explore relationships among plants, place, wellbeing and stewardship without making claims that the evidence cannot support.

PHYSICS

Measure what changes

Light, water, soil, temperature, energy and environmental sensing make change measurable rather than merely picturesque.

SHARED HABIT

Noticing before intervention

Care enough to notice. Measure enough to know. Change carefully enough to learn.

The skill

Noticing is not the opposite of measurement.

Noticing gives us a reason to ask. Measurement helps us decide whether the change we think we see is actually there. Physic(s) Farm treats both as learned practices.

BaselineRepeated viewpoints, field condition, species observations and simple measurements.
InterventionManagement changes documented clearly enough to know what was tried.
ReturnObserve again, compare, revise and share what remains uncertain.
