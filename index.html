Base Lab · Maudy Lane
The beginning

Where attention became evidence.

Base Lab is a pond-side greenhouse at Maudy Lane. It is not a formal laboratory behind a door. It is a place where ordinary observations can become questions worth staying with.

A tadpole becoming a froglet became one small example of the REAL method: notice, return, document, compare and share.

Attention is part of the method: notice, measure, question, return.
NOTICE

What is here that we usually pass by?

RETURN

What changes when we look again?

MEASURE

Evidence

What changed — and how do we know?

Activity alone is not evidence. REAL records what was tried, what people noticed, what measurements showed, what changed and what remains uncertain.

01

Observe

Photographs, measurements, learning traces, field notes and community knowledge establish what is happening.

02

Interpret

We compare, question and verify rather than treating a fluent story or a single metric as proof.

03

Change

Evidence earns its place when it can influence what we do next — including the decision to stop, revise or try again.
