Where attention became evidence.
A small origin story

The question was already waiting in the pond.

A tadpole and later froglet became a small demonstration of the REAL habit: notice something ordinary, stay with it long enough to see change, document carefully and share what was learned.

The significance is not the species itself. It is the practice of attention.

Mathematics is a practice of attention.
We notice patterns, measure change, question evidence and decide what matters.
