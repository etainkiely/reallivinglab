Pattern Commons

Pattern can be invitation, material and expression.
The Pattern Commons

Made by students. Shaped by children. Shared for others to use.

Pattern is not a lesson to complete. Natural materials and patterns can be offered for noticing, arranging, moving, repeating, refusing, changing or returning to.

Nature is the material

Stars, stones, leaves, shells, water, shadow, rhythm and seasonal cycles.

Participation stays open

No requirement to perform, explain or produce a correct pattern.

Change is evidence of listening

What is chosen, repeated, rejected or adapted can inform later sessions and resources.

Pattern Story Cards

Students build possibilities. Children retain ownership of meaning.

Students and artists can create pattern provocations rather than prescribed tasks. A card might begin with a natural pattern and invite someone to notice, choose, make, move or change it.

The educator-facing layer can hold the mathematical ideas, accessibility considerations and observation guardrails — including the instruction not to overinterpret what a child chooses.

An ATU-connected open innovation ecosystem working across community and learning environments in the west of Ireland.
