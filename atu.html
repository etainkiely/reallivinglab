For ATU staff

Bring a real question.

A course, campus space, research challenge or community partnership may suit REAL — but innovation alone does not make an activity a Living Lab.

1 · Real setting

Does the work happen in a context where the outcome matters beyond a demonstration?

2 · Real users

Are the people affected more than subjects, audience or end-point recipients?

3 · Co-creation

Can their knowledge or choices change what is designed or done?

4 · Evidence

Can we show what happened rather than merely report that activity took place?

5 · Iteration

Will the project change after learning — and can that change be documented?

Start small

Bring one question, one setting and one group of real users. REAL can help test whether a Living Lab approach adds genuine value.
