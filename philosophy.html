Our thinking

The practice of attention.

Everything here rests on one practice: paying attention. We form ARKs, small patches returned to flourishing, and we learn to watch nature unfold. Attention turned three ways, toward yourself, toward each other, and toward the living world.

It is the same faculty whether you are watching a tadpole become a frog, steadying your own breathing, or listening to a child who does not use words. Cultivate attention, and the science, the kindness and the making all follow from it.

turned three ways: toward yourself · toward each other · toward the living world
Each site is an ARK, an Act of Restorative Kindness: a patch returned to flourishing, and watched as it does.

Why attention, and not a softer word

Attention is not a mood. It is the single faculty that observation, ethics and design all turn out to share, which is why one practice can hold a whole living lab together. Noticing is attention as science. Restorative kindness is attention as care. And biomimicry is the hinge between the two, the moment where what we have noticed in nature becomes something we make. This is the claim worth standing on in a paper and in front of a European panel.
