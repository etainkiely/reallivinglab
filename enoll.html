Our ENoLL journey

Building the evidence openly.

REAL Living Lab is preparing for external evaluation. ENoLL’s 2026 framework groups 15 criteria into six evaluation chapters. We use those chapters as a public test of our own readiness, showing evidence, work in progress and gaps. Certification will only be displayed if and when it is formally awarded. 1 · Strategy

Is our purpose clear, governed and sustainable? Next: formal governance and multi-year plan.

2 · Operations

Can we repeat the method across projects? Next: formalise the REAL method and project entry process.

3 · Openness

Can people join, challenge and reuse? Next: clearer data, IP and reuse guidance.

4 · Users & Reality

Do real users shape what happens in real settings? Next: systematic You Said → We Changed → We Checked evidence.

5 · Value & Impact

What changed, for whom, and how do we know? Next: consistent longitudinal impact evidence.

6 · Stability & Harmonization

Can REAL persist beyond individual projects? Next: partner commitments, sustainability and responsible scaling.
